📸 Tips for the most accurate results
  • Use even, diffused lighting (natural window light or a lightbox) — avoid direct flash or glare.
  • Shoot straight-on, directly above the card, rather than at an angle.
  • Put the card on a plain, contrasting background so its edges are easy to isolate.
  • Fill the frame with the card, but keep all four edges visible with a small margin.
  • Tap to focus on the card and hold the camera steady before shooting.
  • If you can, remove the card from a sleeve or toploader — glare and reflections reduce accuracy.
  • Use your camera's highest resolution setting; move physically closer rather than zooming digitally.
  • Take separate, clean photos of the front and back — not one photo of both.
